# Trading brief for a second opinion (ASTERUSDT, Bybit perp, 15m) - 2026-10-22

I run a Bollinger-band mean-reversion bot live on Bybit and also trade manually through a Telegram bot. I want a critical second opinion on how to raise the win rate and trade more days without giving up the edge. Please challenge my conclusions, point out overfitting, and suggest tests I have not tried.

## 1. The live strategy ("No.6")
- Symbol ASTERUSDT linear perp, 15m candles, 8x leverage, fixed size $6,000 notional (account about $850-1,300).
- Signal on the last COMPLETED candle: close outside BB(20,2). Close below lower band = long, above upper band = short. Entry at that candle's close (market order).
- Filters: BB width (upper-lower)/mid >= 1.5%; swing gate (no short if close is above the prior 20-bar high, no long if below the prior 20-bar low); crash-wait (no entry for 4 candles = 1 h after a candle with volume >= 4x its 20-bar average and range >= 2 ATR); 1 trade per day per symbol; skip if stop wider than 3% of price.
- Stop: 2.5 x ATR(14). NO fixed take-profit (my rule: let winners run).
- Exit: software ratchet. Once profit reaches 2 ATR, stop moves to entry + 1 ATR; after that it trails 1 ATR behind the best price. Stop is checked first within a candle in backtests.
- Costs modelled: 0.11% round trip fees (0.15% in older tests).
- UNI runs the same rules Mon-Thu 05:30-11:00 WIB (only 15 test trades, less certain).
- Older notes in the trading-brain repo: STRATEGY.md and BACKTEST_LOG.md. Findings there: EMA200 trend filter hurt (PF 0.89), following the trend lost (PF 0.80), the swing gate is required, weekend fade loses.

## 2. What the backtests said (strategy as built, ASTER, replay of the live code)
- Aug+Sep 2026: 33 trades, win 79%, +$1,357 at $8,000 notional.
- October 1-22 at $6,000 (no per-trade loss cap): 14 trades on 14 of 22 days, win 6/14, net -$481, avg win +$40, avg loss -$90. First 8 days +$140, then 8 trades -$621. October is a weak regime for this strategy.
- With a per-trade loss cap of $60 (stop tightened so that loss at the stop <= $60 incl. fees), May-Oct at $6,000: +$2,139 vs +$2,037 without cap, worst trade -$62, drawdown $307 (vs $621).
- About 44% of days in Aug+Sep and 36% in Oct have no strategy trade at all (the 1.5% width filter and crash-wait keep it out).

## 3. My manual trades, 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-22 (14 trades, all ASTER, about $6,000 or $5,000)
- Win 10, loss 4 (71%), net -$5 after fees. Average win +$22.5, average loss -$57.6. Break-even win rate is about 72%, so the edge is about zero despite the high win rate.
- BB width at entry >= 1.5%: 4 trades, 4 wins, +$117. Width < 1.5%: 10 trades, 6 wins, -$122. All four losses were in narrow-band conditions (widths 1.40%, 0.74%, 0.54%, 1.36%).
- Entry with the fade side's band touched in the last 2 candles: 9 trades, 7 wins, +$43. No touch (I entered on feeling): 5 trades, 3 wins, -$48.
- Losers never showed more than about $6 of open profit before the stop; winners had average adverse excursion of about $12. Winners captured about 47% of their peak profit.
- One losing long was entered with price already below the lower band after a 0.8% fall in 2 hours (catching a falling knife).
- A rule "close if no +$10 within 45 minutes" was net about +$15 only (it would have cut one winner), so I dropped it.

## 5. Idea tested: fixed take-profit scalps in sideways hours (08:00-15:00 WIB)
Entry at a band touch, fixed net TP $20-30 on $6,000, stop up to $60. Aug+Sep / Oct net:
- Wick touch, TP $25: -$413 / -$402 (win 62% / 58%, avg win +$25, avg loss -$49 to -$54).
- Close outside band, TP $25: -$66 / -$33. TP $20 and $30 also negative. Tighter stops ($30, $40) also negative (-$125 to -$859).
- Narrow band only (< 1.5%): -$500 / -$357. 24 hours instead of 8-15: about 7 trades per day, -$860 / -$1,251.
- Filling only the no-trade days with such trades: about break-even for "close outside band" (+$6 Aug+Sep with 23 trades, +$85 Oct with 13 trades), negative for wick entries.
Conclusion: a fixed $25 target with $50+ average losses needs about 68% wins, and the simulation gave 58-65%.

## 6. Idea tested: combine the BB signal with other filters (May-Oct, $6,000, $60 loss cap, ratchet exit)
Method: 15m candles, entry at the signal candle's close, ratchet checked on candle highs/lows with stop first, fees 0.11%, crash-wait on. RSI = Wilder RSI(14); ADX(14); "rejection" = wick outside the band but close back inside. Net per period (May-Jul / Aug-Sep / Oct):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. current: close outside band, width >= 1.5% | +868 | +2,005 | +26 | +2,899 |

| Rejection candle only | n/a | -234 | +90 | n/a |

- Win rates of the best candidates are 54-73%. Stacked filters (RSI + ADX + wick + rejection) cut trades to 0.1-0.7 per day and cover only 8-48% of days.
- Trading every day (no filter) covered 100% of days but October was -$249.
- My reading: RSI is the only filter that is positive in all three periods and fixes October a little, but no combination beats the current strategy's total. ADX is unstable (small parameter changes flip the sign). I picked the best rows after seeing them from about 14 variants, so overfitting risk is real.

## 7. Caveats
- One symbol, about 6 months, 15m simulation (not tick data). Stop is assumed hit first when stop and target fall in the same candle. Entry at the candle close ignores slippage.
- Live results are worse than backtests because of fees and slippage. Since going live on 2026-10-17 the ASTER strategy has taken one trade (+$16.7).
- Reported numbers are Aug+Sep (main) and October (recent regime); I deliberately avoid a long-period "yearly profit" headline.
