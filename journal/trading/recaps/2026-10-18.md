## Telegram + LLM assistant (spec for OpenCode)

Owner wants to talk to the bot on Telegram like in VS Code (questions, chart screenshots, manual trades). Decisions: manual trades allowed from Telegram only through a confirmation card (numbers computed by code, tap to execute, no TP, ratchet manages the stop); LLM never touches the exchange; OpenCode builds it from trading-brain `docs/TELEGRAM_LLM.md`. LLM provider still to be chosen by the owner (OpenAI or DeepSeek; screenshots need a vision-capable model).
